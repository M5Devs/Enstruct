You **require** a commercial license if you:

- Use Enstruct in a **closed-source** product or service
- Offer Enstruct (or a derivative) as a **SaaS or hosted service** without releasing your source code
- Integrate Enstruct into a **proprietary product** distributed to end users
- Do not wish to comply with the **AGPL v3** obligations

## Who Does NOT Need a Commercial License?

You can use Enstruct **for free** under AGPL v3 if you:

- Use it for **personal projects** or research
- Use it in another **AGPL v3 open-source project**
- Use it **internally** within your organization without distributing it externally
- Are a **student or educator** using it for non-commercial purposes
